Our Model

“AIRL”

AI In Real Life

Through our in-person event series we want to create the resource for anyone with a baseline level of curiosity and love for creativity, of any skill level, to sit alongside fellow creators to learn about the tools, experiment with them, share about our projects, and demo and discuss the industry where these tools are evolving.

Who We Are

We think of ourselves like an AIRL City where we provide services, activities, and events to support a diversity of needs for the AI community, the AI movement, and the AI creative industry.

Pro Tips

Tips

Pitch your ideas quick! Decide on an idea ASAP
Work together as a team to make a great film
Have fun & make friends!
Learning is the priority
Generate & ideate at the same time
Be careful of: famous people, violence, explicit content, children, blood, etc.
Start editing within an hour
Make clear roles: Editor, Image Generator, etc.
Use chat for prompts if needed
Need help? Raise your hand! Don't be shy!
