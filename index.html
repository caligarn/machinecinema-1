The Format

What is a
GenJam?

Part workshop, part creative sprint, part underground showcase. A GenJam is a live collaborative jam session where artists, filmmakers, and technologists create together using AI — in real time.

Not a panel. Not a lecture. A hands-on creative experiment in human + machine collaboration.

The Flow

Three Phases,
One Session

🧠

Learn

Get a crash course in cutting-edge generative AI tools from expert practitioners.

🎨

Create

Break into teams and make something original — a short film, game trailer, or visual experiment.

🎬

Play

Return to the main room, share your work, and vote for the most inventive creation.

The Schedule

Hour by Hour

Hour 1

Learn

Crash course in a generative AI tool. See what's possible, get hands-on demos, and ask questions.

Hours 2–3

Create

Form small teams with new creative allies. Ideate, generate, iterate. Make something you're proud of.

Hour 4

Play

Screening and showcase. Watch or play each team's creation. Vote. Celebrate. Connect.

After

Connect

New ideas, new skills, new creative allies. Take what you made and keep building.

Phase 1

Learn

Every GenJam begins with a focused tool demo from an expert practitioner. This isn't a sales pitch — it's a working session.

  • Live walkthrough of a generative AI tool
  • Real-time creative demos
  • Tips, tricks, and creative workflows
  • Q&A with the presenter
01
02
Phase 2

Create

This is the heart of the GenJam. Teams form organically, ideas collide, and creations emerge from the chaos.

  • Form teams of 3–5 people
  • 2 hours to ideate, generate, and refine
  • Mentors float between teams
  • AI tools provided and ready to go
Phase 3

Play

The finale. Every team presents their creation to the room. It's part screening, part celebration, part friendly competition.

  • Live screening of all team creations
  • Audience voting for favorites
  • Awards and recognition
  • Networking and celebration
