Pro Tips

Tips

Pitch your ideas quick! Decide on an idea ASAP
Work together as a team to make a great film
Have fun & make friends!
Learning is the priority
Generate & ideate at the same time
Be careful of: famous people, violence, explicit content, children, blood, etc.
Start editing within an hour
Make clear roles: Editor, Image Generator, etc.
Use chat for prompts if needed
